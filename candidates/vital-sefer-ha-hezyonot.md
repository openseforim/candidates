Five parts in the printed editions: (1) a diary of events, (2–3) dreams, (4) *Kuntres Shoresh Nishmati*, (5) a mixed part, only partly by Vital, which Faierstein leaves out. Y. Avivi argues that the book is two separate works: the diary with its dreams (parts 1–3) and the *Kuntres*.

Copyright: Vital's text is old, but the full text exists only in modern editions. Aescoly died in 1948 (check), so his 1954 edition is likely public domain in the EU, but probably not in the US (an Israeli book of 1954). The 1826/27 printing is public domain but cut and censored. Options: a reader of the 1826/27 text with the missing passages marked; or a new transcription from the autograph photograph, if a scan of it can be found. Do not copy Aescoly's or Faierstein's notes, and do not use Faierstein's translation. Suggested by the owner, 26-Nov-2026.
