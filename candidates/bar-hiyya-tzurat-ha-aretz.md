---
title: Abraham bar Ḥiyya, *Tzurat ha-Aretz*
title_he: צורת הארץ
author: Abraham bar Ḥiyya ha-Nasi (c. 1065–c. 1136)
published: Basel 1546 (Hebrew with a Latin translation by Sebastian Münster, *Sphaera mundi*; part only); first complete printing Offenbach 1720 (with diagrams)
language: Hebrew (Basel 1546 adds Latin)
status: proposed
added: 26-Nov-2026
checked: 26-Nov-2026: web search for editions and translations, archive.org
why: The first Hebrew cosmography and geography: the shape of the earth, the climes and the inhabited world, and the structure and motions of the heavenly spheres, written in Barcelona for readers without Arabic. It set the Hebrew terms for astronomy that later works, down to *Ayil Meshulash*, still use.
scans: Basel 1546: [archive.org bub_gb_WHz_pQCIwGoC](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_WHz_pQCIwGoC) (Google Books scan). Offenbach 1720: check HebrewBooks and the NLI (copies at auction). The companion work, *Ḥeshbon Mahalakhot ha-Kokhavim*, survives too.
existing: No English translation found (only an excerpt in the Posen Library, as *The Form of the Earth and the Structure of the Heavenly Orbs*). Latin: Münster 1546 (part). Spanish: J. M. Millás Vallicrosa may have translated it (check).
