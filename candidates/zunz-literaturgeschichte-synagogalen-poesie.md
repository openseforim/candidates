A reference work, not a narrative: a reader with a searchable index of poets and first lines may be worth more than a running translation. Pairs with the companion volumes *Die synagogale Poesie des Mittelalters* (1855) and *Die Ritus des synagogalen Gottesdienstes* (1859).
