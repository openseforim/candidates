why: The travel account of a Jerusalem emissary in 1858–63: Egypt, Yemen, Aden, India, Java, Australia and New Zealand, with the history, customs, manuscripts and conditions of the Jews in each place. The first detailed outside account of Yemenite Jewry, and a much-used source for it.
scans: Lyck 1866 and Mainz 1874: Open Library [OL23298631M](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL23298631M) and [OL50256091M](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL50256091M) (check for full-view scans); check archive.org, HathiTrust and HebrewBooks.

English: only selections, from the chapters on Australia and New Zealand, by Rabbi L. A. Falk of Sydney ([OzTorah](https://oztorah.com/?p=9077)). Later Hebrew editions (one edited by A. Ya'ari) may be in copyright; do not copy their notes.
