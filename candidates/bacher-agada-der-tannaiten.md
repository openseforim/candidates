status: researched

scans: 2nd ed. vol. 1 (1903) and vol. 2: [archive.org dieagadadertanna01bach](https://archive.org/details/dieagadadertanna01bach), [archive.org dieagadadertanna02bach](https://archive.org/details/dieagadadertanna02bach) (Toronto, 400–500 ppi).
