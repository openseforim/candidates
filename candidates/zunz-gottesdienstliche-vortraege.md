why: The founding work of the Wissenschaft des Judentums: the first historical account of the midrash and the synagogue sermon, from the Bible to Zunz's own day. Still the starting point for the dating and history of the midrashic collections, and never translated into English.

Base text: the 1892 edition, which adds Brüll's notes from Zunz's own copy (both men died long ago). German with many Hebrew quotations on the same lines, so plan for a vision model or a two-pass OCR. Do not use Albeck's Hebrew additions.
