title: Estori ha-Parḥi, *Kaftor va-Feraḥ*
title_he: כפתור ופרח
author: Ishtori (Estori) ha-Parḥi (1280–c. 1355)
published: written in Beit She'an, finished 1322; Venice 1549; ed. Z. H. Edelmann, Berlin 1852; ed. A. M. Luncz, Jerusalem 1897–99
language: Hebrew
status: proposed
added: 26-Nov-2026
checked: 26-Nov-2026: Hebrew Wikisource (from the Wikisource inventory), English Wikipedia, web search for translations
why: The first Hebrew geography of the Land of Israel, by a Provençal exile who travelled the country for seven years: sites identified with places in the Bible and the Talmud, plants, fruits, coins, weights and measures, and the laws that apply only in the Land. A primary source for Talmudic realia.
scans: Venice 1549 (check HebrewBooks and the NLI). Edelmann, Berlin 1852: Google Books 99xAAQAAMAAJ. Luncz, Jerusalem 1897–99: Google Books K4JTAAAAYAAJ. Check archive.org and HathiTrust for full-view copies.
existing: Hebrew Wikisource has only a stub ([כפתור ופרח](https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/כפתור_ופרח): the title page and the contents). No English translation known: a Biblical Archaeology Society note says none exists ([endnote](https://library.biblicalarchaeology.org/?p=47560)). Studies: Rosenson and Glicksberg (eds.), *Rabbi Ishtori ha-Parḥi* (Efrata College, 2015); Shneor, *Cathedra* 143 (2012). A modern annotated Hebrew edition by A. Y. Havatzelet (Jerusalem, 1990s–2000s) may exist (check); it is in copyright.
