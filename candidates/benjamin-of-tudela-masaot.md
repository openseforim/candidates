why: The best-known medieval Hebrew travel book: the Jewish communities from Spain to Baghdad and beyond, with their numbers and leaders, in the 1160s–70s. Short and much cited.
scans: Adler 1907: [HebrewBooks 20762](https://www.hebrewbooks.org/20762) (223 pp.; Hebrew text from British Museum MS 27089, with variants from the Casanatense, Oxford, Epstein and Bodleian MSS and the Constantinople and Ferrara editions, the English translation, notes and facsimiles). Check archive.org and HathiTrust for a cleaner scan of the same book.
existing: English: Adler 1907 (public domain; Adler died 1911) and A. Asher, London 1840–41 (public domain); both are widely online. Check Hebrew Wikisource and Sefaria for the Hebrew text.
