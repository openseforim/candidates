Bornstein died in 1928, so the article is public domain in the EU; it appeared before 1931 (if in *Ha-Tekufah*, about 1922), so also in the US. His other calendar works: *Maḥloket Rav Sa'adyah Ga'on u-Ben Me'ir* (Warsaw 1904, [HebrewBooks 46973](https://beta.hebrewbooks.org/46973)), *Peletah mini Kedem* (Harkavy jubilee volume, St Petersburg 1909), *Ḥikrei Olamim* (Warsaw 1927) and *Ha-Tekufot ve-Hitpatteḥutan* (Poznański memorial volume, Warsaw 1927).
