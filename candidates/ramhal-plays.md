One candidate for the three plays, since each is short. A first reader could be *La-Yesharim Tehillah*, the best known and the one with the most public-domain printings; *Migdal Oz* is the longest. Base texts: the 1743 Amsterdam edition if a scan is found, otherwise a 19th-century reprint; Letteris 1837 for *Migdal Oz* (all editors died long ago). Ginzburg (died 1944) is public domain in the EU; his 1927 volumes were published before 1931 and so are public domain in the US, though HathiTrust still shows them as US access only. Use the 1949 and 1972 editions only to check readings. *Leshon Limmudim* (Mantua 1727), Ramḥal's treatise on rhetoric, is the work the Samson play was written to illustrate, and is a related candidate.
