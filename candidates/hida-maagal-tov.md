Base text so far: the 1885 printing (the reader started with it, 24-Nov-2026), because the HebrewBooks scan of Freimann's edition gave 26% wrong words with Kraken. Freimann's edition remains the target for the full text. Freimann died in 1948, so the edition is public domain in the EU (from 2019); part 1 (1921) is public domain in the US, while HathiTrust treats the 1934 volume as in copyright in the US. The diary text itself is 18th-century. Do not copy readings, notes or added passages from the 2022 editions. The 1885 printing has only part of the text but a clean full-view scan with an OCR layer, good for an OCR test. His son Isaiah held the diary back from print because it spoke badly of living people; the diary records his bouts of melancholy. Suggested in the review of the Emden reader.
