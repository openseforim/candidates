I. Kaempf, Berlin 1845 ([archive.org](https://archive.org/details/dieerstenmakamen00juda)). Lagarde or Kaminka would be the base text.
existing: English translations by V. E. Reichert (1965–73) and D. S. Segal (2001), both in copyright: the value is an open Hebrew text and an open translation. The critical edition by Yahalom and Katsumata (2010) is in copyright and must not be used. Project Ben-Yehuda has the whole vocalized Hebrew text (checked 23-Nov-2026), so no OCR is needed.
