checked: 24-Nov-2026: archive.org, Open Library, the HathiTrust volumes API, Sefaria, Wikisource (de, he), the Frankfurt and Potsdam digital collections and the Project Ben-Yehuda catalogue
why: The English *History of the Jews* (JPS, 1891–98) translates the narrative but leaves out the *Noten*, the long source-critical excursuses at the end of each volume. They are where Graetz argues his dates and readings, and they are still cited. They have never been in English.
scans: Every volume is on archive.org, for example the 1900 edition from Illinois ([vol. 10](https://archive.org/details/geschichtederjud10grae_0)) and the Toronto set ([vol. 10](https://archive.org/details/geschichtederjud10grae)).
existing: English: the JPS abridgement (6 vols.) without the notes, public domain. Hebrew: *Divre yeme Yisra'el*, trans. S. P. Rabinowitz (Warsaw, 1890s), with notes; check its status. Not on Wikisource.
