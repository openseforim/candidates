scans: Kahana, Kiev 1911–12, 71 pp.: [archive.org sepherhayyeyehud01mode](https://archive.org/details/sepherhayyeyehud01mode) (Duke University Libraries, 360 ppi); [archive.org 3855186.0001.001.umich.edu](https://archive.org/details/3855186.0001.001.umich.edu) (University of Michigan). The autograph manuscript is in the Bodleian Library, Oxford.
existing: Project Ben-Yehuda has the whole text from Kahana's edition (work 31022, about 230 KB), so no OCR is needed; check it against the scans. English: M. R. Cohen, *The Autobiography of a Seventeenth-Century Venetian Rabbi* (Princeton, 1988), with notes by H. E. Adelman and B. C. I. Ravid, in copyright. The critical edition by D. Carpi (Tel Aviv, 1985) is in copyright and must not be used.
